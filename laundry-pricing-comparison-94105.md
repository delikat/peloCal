## 3. Summary

### Who Should Choose Mulberrys?

- **Everyday dry cleaning** (shirts, pants, blazers, suits, coats) -- generally $1-5/item cheaper
- **Pay-as-you-go wash & fold** -- flat bag pricing ($30/$60) with free delivery is a much better deal than Rinse's per-pound + fees model
- You value **faster turnaround** (1-day standard vs. 3-4 days)
- You prefer **no delivery fees** ever
- You want **in-store drop-off** option (244 Townsend St near 94105)

### Who Should Choose Rinse?

- You need **formal dresses** dry cleaned ($34.95 vs. $59.99 -- a $25 savings)
- You need **ties and scarves** cleaned ($6.95 vs. $12.99)
- You want the **Rinse Repeat subscription** for predictable per-pound pricing at $1.39-1.64/lb
- You want **free button repair** with dry cleaning
- You prefer an **app-based experience** with itemized costs shown before ordering
- You do laundry in **small, frequent loads** where per-pound pricing is advantageous

### Overall

For ZIP 94105, **Mulberrys tends to be the more cost-effective choice** for most common dry cleaning items and especially for pay-as-you-go wash & fold. Rinse's strength is its subscription model for wash & fold (Rinse Repeat) and specific item categories where it undercuts Mulberrys. Both offer free pickup/delivery (Mulberrys always; Rinse with subscription), eco-friendly cleaning, and good SF coverage.
